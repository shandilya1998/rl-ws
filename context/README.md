# Context Index

This directory holds the accumulated factual record of the workspace, the documents that establish what the codebase does, what the experiments measured, and what the literature prescribes. A context document is descriptive rather than prescriptive, it records findings that have been verified against the live sources, the dumped run configurations, the TensorBoard logs, or the published literature, and it carries file and line citations so that a later reader need not repeat the investigation. Documents that propose changes rather than record facts live in [../plans](../plans/README.md) instead.

The purpose of this index is to let a reader, whether human or agent, determine which documents bear on a question and in what order to read them, without opening any of them speculatively. Each entry below states the subject, the currency of the contents, and the specific questions the document answers.

## Reading protocol

Begin with [knowledge_base.md](knowledge_base.md), which is the hub of the co-optimisation investigation and carries the objectives, the entry point file map, and the consolidated findings of every investigation wave. It also records the later work streams in summary, so it will indicate which of the specialised documents a given question requires.

Thereafter read only the documents named in the register below as bearing on the subject at hand. The specialised documents were written by parallel investigation agents against disjoint areas, so they overlap very little, and reading one rarely obliges reading another. Where two documents disagree, the later dated entry governs, and the currency column records which documents contain claims that the codebase has since overtaken.

Before modifying any shared module, consult the document covering that module rather than re-reading the source, and record any newly discovered fact in the relevant document so that the next session inherits it. This obligation is codified in [../CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md).

### brs_gait.md

The running record of the SD_BRS1 gait quality investigation, and the most frequently amended document in the workspace. It advances in numbered passes, each pass analysing a training run through video frames, TensorBoard scalars, and the run's own dumped configuration, and each pass superseding the narrative of its predecessors where the evidence demanded. Its principal results are the discovery that the foot clearance reward measured the ankle frame height and was therefore farmed by tilting a grounded foot, the two successive remedies of a contact gate and then a true sole clearance term, the identification of a permanent shank into foot self penetration in the URDF as the root cause of hopping and dragging, the reward budget inversions computed as rates per second divided by weights, and the finding that the all zero nominal pose rested on the knee hard limit at the straight leg singularity. It closes with an experiment history table covering twelve runs from 2026-07-15 to 2026-07-30, which is the fastest way to learn what each configuration achieved. Its twentieth pass, added on 2026-07-31, is the first to work from the `play.py` numpy dumps directly rather than from TensorBoard scalars and video frames, and it analyses four runs together as an ablation already performed. Two of its results are load bearing well beyond the immediate question. The articulation joint and body order is left before right rather than right before left, proved from the joint limits, so the `JOINT_NAMES` and `FEET_NAMES` tables in the dashboard are transposed and every per joint plot this project has exported carries swapped side labels. And the `feet_distance` reward hinges on the planar separation of the feet, which a long stride satisfies while the lateral separation is zero, so stance width has never in fact been regulated by it.

Its twenty-first and twenty-second passes carry the outcomes of the phased remedy that the twentieth pass motivated. The twenty-first records that Phase 1 failed and dissects three defects in the landing term it wired, that the term is a time integral which a policy minimises by loitering high rather than by landing softly, that its height gate is a frame proxy overestimating the true sole clearance by 23.5 millimetres, and that it charges the frame velocity rather than the sole approach velocity, which the policy exploited by rotating its foot into the ground. The twenty-second records that the corrected Phases 1b and 2 both passed, halving the peak contact force and the cost of transport while raising double support from 0.84 percent to 11.62, and that the policy paid for this by straightening its legs, which migrated the sagittal work to the hip and cost the gait the compliance to absorb a disturbance. Four findings there are load bearing beyond the immediate question. `NoFlyWithGrace` is saturated at 99.8 percent, so its coefficient cannot discriminate single from double support and is not a lever. `feet_air_time_positive_biped` gates its whole reward on single stance, so it and not the single support term is what prices the weight transfer interval out, at 2.741 per second. The impact and steady stance force distributions now overlap, which fixes 800 newtons as the floor for any collision threshold. And the tracking error metrics do not converge downward while a curriculum is active, so runs must be compared at matched iteration counts with the curriculum values verified equal.

Its twenty-fourth pass, added on 2026-08-05, reads run `2026-08-03_11-19-11` at its completion of 29103 iterations, the twenty-second pass having read the same run at 11000 with the budget confound qualifying every figure. Three of its results revise the record. Phase 2 must be recorded as FAILED, its double support fraction having reached 11.615 percent at 11000 iterations and decayed to 7.858 by the end against a gate of 12, so the provisional pass of the twenty-second pass is withdrawn. The set point clearance term is shown to be an attractor rather than merely a defective maximiser, its training income rising 36 percent over exactly the interval in which every measure of swing shape deteriorated, while its value reconstructed from kinematics is a third lower on the better profile than on the two worse ones, so the term pays more for the plateau and the policy climbs toward it whenever budget remains. And the completed run's play dump is contaminated by three tree weights that moved between the two evaluations, which the twenty-second pass had been able to exempt itself from, detected by dividing a logged reward by the value reconstructed from kinematics and recovering an implied weight of 19.98 against a trained 12.0. That division is the general method and should precede any reading of a reward channel. The pass further records that the 11000 iteration dump was overwritten in place and no longer exists, that the impact threshold floor moves from 800 to 850 newtons as the stance force distribution has risen, and that the tracking caution of the twenty-second pass is reversed, this run having improved its planar error from 0.218 to 0.182 against a tightening curriculum.

Its twenty-third pass, added on 2026-08-04, computes no gait result and treats the instrument instead, having been opened because every pass since the twentieth re-derived its measurements in a script that did not survive. Three of its findings govern the reading of the whole record. The per term series in a reward dump is a rate per second while the total is a per step value, the ratio between them being exactly the control period, so a budget mixing the two is wrong by a factor of one hundred. A symmetry index pooled across environments cancels the asymmetry it is meant to expose and must be formed per environment, which resolves what would otherwise look like a discrepancy against the twentieth pass. And the `SOLE_OFFSETS` guard in `play.py` tests a table literal rather than the configured global, so the SD_BRS1 sole geometry is applied to whatever robot is played.

### copt_ppo_nonstationarity.md

The mathematical treatment of the design swap, written to settle whether replacing every robot in the scene every two hundred and forty iterations is compatible with proximal policy optimisation and whether it explains the training variance that objective 1 of the knowledge base has left open. It proves that the association of a design to an environment index carries no information the optimiser consumes, by walking the four places the environment axis appears and showing each to be invariant under a permutation of it, and it establishes that no policy update ever straddles a swap, so the two failure modes a reader would most reasonably fear are absent. Its section 6 then draws the distinction the whole question turns on, between an estimator that scatters around a fixed target and an estimand that moves beneath an accurate estimator, and works the distinction through two designs from the actual lattice to show that the base height penalty prescribes a crouch for a long legged design, full extension for the nominal one, and nothing at all for a short legged one whose target is unreachable, so that the per-design gradients genuinely differ and a reweighting of the population genuinely changes the correct answer.

Its section 3.1 records the finding that governs the rest, that the two decimal rounding in `_compute_link_extents` makes the design space a finite lattice of at most about one hundred and ninety five points of which roughly eighty are realised, which is smaller than the population of two hundred and fifty six, so every generation contains duplicate designs, no design is ever novel after the opening generations, and the early population is a single design repeated because the growing spread is initially below the quantisation. From this it derives the inference that decides the investigation, that the estimand displacement is bounded by the total variation distance between consecutive populations and must therefore vanish as the search distribution collapses, so a variance which persists after that collapse cannot be caused by it.

The document accordingly places its weight on a mechanism driven by the cadence rather than by design diversity, that the bare environment reset inside the morphology update zeroes the episode counter of all four thousand and ninety six environments at once, so a rollout thereafter samples a twenty five step slice of episode phase rather than the stationary occupancy measure. Section 7 gives this the full treatment, the role of `episode_length_buf` as the state variable the timeout termination reads, what `init_at_random_ep_len` does and the fact that it works forward in time by staggering future resets rather than present states, the exact design effect formula relating the variance inflation to the fraction of return variance that phase explains, a table putting four thousand and ninety six environments at the statistical worth of ninety eight for a correlation of one percent, a worked computation showing the velocity tracking term swinging by a factor of seven and a half between a standing start and cruise, and the argument that the lock does not decay because a timeout returns an environment to the cohort it left and only the twelve percent of episodes ending in a fall reschedule it. Its section 7.7 records a coupling no prior document notes, that the angular command curriculum's evaluation interval of six thousand control steps exactly equals the morphology cadence, so that gate is evaluated only ever at swap boundaries and only ever on truncated episode sums, the terrain suppression flag not extending to it.

Two further sections carry weight. Section 8 revises the value baseline mechanism downward on the lattice finding, since a critic that has seen all eighty designs for thousands of iterations is not extrapolating, while recording that `compute_returns_design_wise` has no caller anywhere in the live tree and withdrawing the claim the 2026-07-30 audit made that it had landed. And section 12 orders six remedies, noting for the first of them that re-randomising the episode counter would create spurious short episodes which the fitness accumulator would count, so the same change must gate fitness on episodes that began after the swap.

## [bandit_design_optimisation.md](bandit_design_optimisation.md)

Written 2026-10-01 and expanded the same day at the user's request. A literature survey and idea generation report asking whether the theory of multi-armed bandits, and in particular a bandit whose decision rule is a neural network, can serve as the design search of the co-optimisation pipeline, where a design comprises link lengths, a nominal pose and an actuator per joint group, and, as a follow-up, how proximal policy optimisation could be modified so that the design bandit becomes part of its own formulation. Its subject spans the runner and design generator of the simulation repository, the vendored Isaac Lab action and observation terms, and a body of external literature, so it sits at the workspace level. Its one hundred and twenty eight references are registered as cluster 33 of [literature.md](literature.md), whose closing paragraph records three corrections found on reading the full texts.

It is written to be read without the surveyed papers. A notation table opens it, and its section 2 derives the probability tools every later argument uses, concentration from Markov's inequality to Hoeffding's, relative entropy and the Fisher information, conjugate and Gaussian conditioning, the score function identity with its baseline, and the natural gradient. Sections 3 to 6 then build the theory with proofs, the finite-armed bandit with its two lower bounds, the full UCB and Exp3 analyses, posterior sampling's reduction to optimism, the gradient bandit and its recent convergence results, pure exploration and sequential halving, and LinUCB with the elliptical potential lemma, followed by continuous spaces through discretisation, zooming, HOO, the four-lemma GP-UCB proof and CMA-ES as an information-geometric natural gradient, hybrid spaces through CatCMA's derivation and margin, and neural bandits through the neural tangent kernel, NeuralUCB, ensembles, the epinet and sample-then-optimise Thompson sampling.

Five of its results are load bearing beyond the survey. The incumbent CMA-ES and CatCMA searches are themselves gradient bandits on a search distribution, and a network that predicts a design without any input is only a costlier parameterisation of the same family, so a network earns its place solely as a reward model, as a context conditioned generator, or as a multimodal one. In the concurrent loop an arm's value depends on its own pull history, the shared policy improving fastest on the designs it trains upon, which is the bandit statement of the first-mover advantage Mertan and Cheney describe and of the bias ECoDe's authors observed in a universal policy. The design-conditioned critic is a neural reward model the pipeline already trains, and the one published design method whose critic is an ensemble of heads uses only their mean, guarding against extrapolation with a distance penalty, so no retrieved work uses such a critic's uncertainty to direct exploration. Proximal policy optimisation run on a bandit lets each arm's probability move by at most its old probability times the clipping range, a proven route to locking onto the initially favoured arm, which bears directly on a design head initialised as the incumbent is with half of each group's probability on the donor's actuator. And the nominal pose enters the action offset, the relative joint observation and the joint deviation penalty, the offset being refreshed by the respawn pathway alone, so it cannot be opened as an arm until the three requirements of its section 11.7 and the design-invariant reward of the audit's section 4.8 are met.

Its section 9 answers the follow-up. It derives proximal policy optimisation from the policy gradient theorem and the trust region bound, shows that a bandit is a one-step instance of the problem the algorithm solves, surveys the works that place the design inside the objective, Transform2Act, BodyGen with its design-stage Monte Carlo advantage and Stackelberg proximal policy optimisation with its implicit controller-response gradient, and those that steer the algorithm from outside with a bandit, and proposes a generation-level design head trained by a clipped surrogate with a critic baseline, an entropy bonus, a relative entropy anchor to the donor-centred distribution and a floored categorical head. Its section 12 ranks six candidate contributions and recommends beginning with a critic-seeded neural Thompson sampler benchmarked against both the incumbent and random search, with the generation-level design head as its natural ablation.
