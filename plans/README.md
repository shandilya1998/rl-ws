# Plans Index

This directory holds the prescriptive documents of the workspace, the designs and implementation plans that propose changes to the codebase. A plan document states a problem, grounds it in evidence, surveys whatever literature bears on it, and then sets out the concrete edits that realise the remedy, ordinarily at the granularity of a named file and a named symbol. Documents that record established facts rather than proposed changes live in [../context](../context/README.md) instead.

Every plan here has been checked against the live sources, and the register below states for each one whether its proposals are implemented, partially implemented, superseded, or still outstanding. That status is the single most useful thing a reader can know about a plan, since a plan whose proposals have landed is a historical record of why the code looks as it does, while a plan still outstanding is a specification of work to be done.

## Reading protocol

Consult the register first and read the status column before the document. A plan marked as implemented should be read for its justification, not for its instructions, and its instructions should never be applied a second time. A plan marked as outstanding may be read as a specification and executed, subject to the change discipline in [../CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md), which requires that backwards compatibility be preserved absolutely and that a new behaviour be carried by an optional argument whose default reproduces the old behaviour exactly.

Each plan grounds itself in one or more context documents, and those are named in the summaries below. Read the grounding context before executing a plan, because the plans quote line numbers that were correct when written and have since drifted, whereas the context documents identify the symbols by name.

The two documents at the workspace root, [../ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md) and [../CO_OPTIMISATION.md](../CO_OPTIMISATION.md), describe the project as it stands and are the correct reference for the architecture a plan modifies.

## Document summaries

### COPT_INVESTIGATION_PLAN.md

The consolidated remedy for the three deficiencies of the co-optimisation pipeline that the investigation recorded in `../context/knowledge_base.md` identified, namely the persistently high variance of the training curves, the redundant repetition of the first several thousand iterations on every run, and the biased low variety sampling of the design optimiser. Section 2 specifies a complete checkpoint, enumerating every item of runner, algorithm, generator, and environment manager state that a resume must restore, and prescribing a new environment state module to capture and restore it. Section 3 specifies the CMA-ES initialisation, reducing the initial step size, widening the parameter ranges, correcting the ordering of the tell and ask calls, and removing the two decimal rounding that quantised designs below a centimetre. Section 4 specifies three variance remedies, discarding truncated returns at the morphology update rather than flooding the reward window with them, suppressing the terrain curriculum update during a morphology reset, and normalising advantages within each individual's block of environments rather than globally.

This document was formerly named `TODO.md`. It has been renamed because the original name did not distinguish it from a scratch list, and because it is referenced by name from the architecture documents.

Its status is implemented but for one item. The complete checkpoint exists, as `save` and `load` overrides on the runner backed by `co_optimisation/utils/env_state.py`. The CMA-ES initialisation has been changed as prescribed, the initial step size is now 0.25, the parameter ranges are now bounded to 0.75 and 1.25, and the generator class is now imported correctly. All three variance remedies have landed, truncated returns are discarded at the morphology update, the delayed terrain curriculum skips its update while a morphology reset is in progress, and per individual advantage normalisation is performed by the co-optimisation algorithm. The evolutionary schedule has additionally been retuned beyond what the plan proposed, to 256 individuals at an update interval of 240 iterations with the random phase extended to 12000 iterations. The one outstanding item is the removal of the two decimal rounding of link extents, which the plan recommended and which remains in the design generator.

### COPT_LEARNED_MODEL.md

The design and implementation plan for the learned model extension of the co-optimisation pipeline, exposed as the policy type COPT-LEARNED. It proposes an encoder decoder estimator trained jointly with PPO under a single optimiser, in which the encoder consumes the privileged morphology and terrain observations together with a flattened proprioceptive history and produces a latent that conditions the actor, while a decoder regresses the robot's ground truth dynamic state, the torques, accelerations, inertia, contact forces, and foot velocities, from that same latent. The regression adds a mean squared error term to the objective, so the latent is compelled to encode the dynamics rather than merely whatever the policy gradient happens to find useful. Section 1 makes the case from sample complexity, section 2 gives the formulation, the network architecture, a comparison against the hybrid internal model architecture, and the hyperparameters, and section 3 is the implementation plan in twelve numbered steps, each naming its file.

Section 3 was implemented in full on 2026-07-03, following every step and cross checking against the live sources rather than against the line numbers quoted in the document. The implementation record, including the two ambiguities that had to be resolved, is in `../context/copt.md` section 9. The codebase has since grown a second variant beyond this plan, comprising `CoptLearnedModelV2ActorCritic` and `CoptLearnedModelV2PPO`, which differ in deriving the estimator input from the observation history alone, and that variant is not described by this document nor by any other. Note also that the variant is not presently reachable, because the branch in the training entry point that would select it is guarded by a condition admitting only the two earlier policy types.

### SYMMETRY_PLAN.md

The complete implementation brief for enforcing left right symmetry on the SD_BRS1 biped through the symmetry hook that the vendored reinforcement learning library already exposes. It grounds the problem in the asymmetric limping gait of a specific run, frames the mathematics as an order two reflection group under which the optimal policy is equivariant and the optimal value invariant, and catalogues the four practical mechanisms the literature offers before selecting data augmentation. It then carries the whole codebase investigation as validation material, the consumer contract of the library hook, the configuration surface and the argument that no configuration class need change, the mapping from the reference implementation, and the observation and action layout of the robot term by term. The implementation plan supplies the robot specific mirror module in full, its wiring, a mandatory runtime confirmation of the collision shape count that the mirror cannot infer statically, and a self test to run before launching.

The plan is implemented. The mirror module exists as `mdp/symmetry/brs.py` and is wired into the agent configuration, and the run that first enabled it produced the first policy in the SD_BRS1 stream to walk with a coordinated alternating gait. A point the plan itself raised late and which the context record confirms is that the critic group must be mirrored as well as the policy group, since a value function that observes an unmirrored privileged state cannot be invariant under the reflection.

### NATURAL_GAIT_PLAN.md

The investigation and phased plan addressing three defects of the first successful SD_BRS1 walking policy, a stiff straight legged gait in which the knees barely move, a forward inclination of the torso, and a lack of smoothness in the torque and joint trajectories. It is a running record rather than a fixed plan, each phase adding its measured outcome and revising what follows. Its analytical contribution is considerable, it builds the reward budget of the trained run as rates per second divided by weights, performs a post mortem establishing that the first knee flexion reward was mathematically dead because its Gaussian kernel was placed so far from the operating point that its gradient was of order ten to the minus six, identifies a stale contact index in the airborne and single support gates, demonstrates that the base height target lay outside the reachable pose set, and derives a feasible crouch together with the sign convention of the nominal pose.

Phase A, the swing phase knee flexion reward, is marked superseded within the document itself, having failed in the run that trained it. Phase A2, which instead moves the nominal pose to the derived crouch and corrects the terms that reference it, is implemented, and the run that trained it walks with alternating feet and bent knees. Phase B, restoring an upright torso, and Phase C, smoothing the torque and joint trajectories, remain outstanding. The two residual defects observed after Phase A2, a narrow stance and a hip roll oscillation, are analysed at the end of `../context/brs_gait.md` rather than here.

### GAIT_EFFICIENCY_PLAN.md

The successor to `NATURAL_GAIT_PLAN.md`, taking up the defects that remained once the policy that plan produced actually walked. Its subject is the quality of the gait rather than its existence, and its evidence is unusually strong, being computed from the `play.py` numpy dumps of four runs trained from near identical configurations and evaluated on the same day, which constitute an ablation already performed, rather than inverted from logged reward rates.

Its analytical contribution is to establish that the gait tracks its command through a manner of walking that would not survive hardware, striking the ground at 3.08 body weights on the average step and 13.76 at the worst, descending onto it at 1.68 metres per second which is 1.26 times free fall from the swing apex, pressing its ankle roll actuators onto their effort ceilings for six to seven percent of every second while standing within two hundredths of a radian of a mechanical stop for a third of it, and circulating 850 watts of joint power to deliver 89, for a mechanical cost of transport of 2.38 against the 0.2 of human walking. It then reduces those observations to five causes, each of which is a property of the reward specification rather than of the learning. The clearance reward's maximiser over a swing of fixed duration is a trapezoid, which earns forty six percent more than a sinusoidal arc of the same apex and is paid for by nothing. The single support terms price the weight transfer interval at zero, so that although the gait clock commands a double support fraction of 19.6 percent, close to the human 24, the policy delivers 2.3, and compliance is worth only three percent of the positive budget, which is to say the schedule is not really specified. The effort and smoothness terms sit at six percent of income, of which five sixths is one term, so leaning on a mechanical stop is cheaper than controlling the joint. The feet distance hinge measures the planar separation, which a long stride satisfies while the feet cross the midline beneath it. And the yaw authority of a machine whose hip yaw joints are declared fixed is spent by a narrow stance, a lateral command range of a hundredth of a metre per second, a slide penalty on the very stance pivot that turning requires, and the absence of any foot yaw term.

The plan is six phases ordered by cost. Pricing the impact comes first, since it is the cheapest and addresses the defect the user identified, and it needs only two weight changes, the wiring of a `foot_landing_vel` term that already exists in the repository and is already used by a sibling task, and one new threshold hinge on contact force. Restoring the double support interval follows through a grace window of the kind van Marum and colleagues carry. The swing profile is then respecified against the half sinusoid reference that the gait command already declares through its unread `swing_height` parameter. Stance width, yaw authority, and actuation smoothness follow. Every weight it proposes is derived from a measured term value rather than from an analogy, and the caller set of every function it touches was enumerated against the live sources on the day it was written.

The document also records an instrumentation defect that governs the reading of every per joint plot this project has exported. The articulation joint and body order is left before right, not right before left, proved decisively by the fact that joint index 2 spans a range only `HipPitchL` admits while index 3 spans one only `HipPitchR` admits, so the `JOINT_NAMES` and `FEET_NAMES` tables in the dashboard are transposed.

Revised on 2026-08-03 with the outcome of Phase 1, trained as run `2026-07-31_10-21-10`, which failed its gate and is dissected in the new section 4.1a. Only two of the phase's three changes had been applied, the clearance weight reduction having been omitted, and the marginal arithmetic computed over the dump shows that under the configuration actually trained a softer landing was a net loss of 0.81 per second, so the policy was correct to keep stomping. Three further defects were found in `foot_landing_vel` itself, that it is a time integral which the policy minimised by loitering high rather than by landing softly, that its height gate is the frame proxy which overestimates the true sole clearance by 23.5 millimetres and stood shut on a third of the low foot steps, and that it charges the frame velocity rather than the sole approach velocity, which the policy exploited by rotating its foot into the ground and paid for by driving the ankle pitch to its effort ceiling. Section 4.1b specifies the corrected retry around a new `foot_landing_vel_v2`. Two instrumentation findings recorded there govern the reading of every play dump, that `play.py` rebuilds its configuration from the live tree so a dump's reward weights are not the training weights, and that the dump's instantaneous contact force understates the true peak by an order of magnitude because the impact term reads the sensor's force history. The document further corrects its own Phase 3 reference from a half sinusoid, which would have commanded a 0.66 metre per second touchdown, to a raised cosine, and corrects the commanded double support of a 0.6 stance duration from 19.6 to 20.0 percent.

Revised again on 2026-08-04 with the outcome of Phases 1b and 2, trained together as run `2026-08-03_11-19-11`, which met both gates at a third of the training budget its predecessor consumed and is recorded in the new section 4.2a. The mean peak contact force fell from 2.19 body weights to 1.58 and the sole approach speed at touchdown from 1.86 metres per second to 0.71, double support rose from 0.84 percent to 11.62, the cost of transport fell from 3.34 to 1.87, and the ankle saturation that Phase 1 introduced was eliminated on both axes. The swing profile became single peaked without Phase 3 acting, its apex moving to 0.463 of the swing and its correlation against the raised cosine reaching 0.917. Two regressions came with the gains and share one cause, that the policy straightened its legs, the stance knee flexion falling to under two degrees, which migrated the sagittal work to a hip pitch actuator now at 82 percent of its ceiling and left the gait without the compliance to absorb a disturbance, the low height terminations nearly doubling at the matched iteration.

The same pass wires Phase 3 and derives, in the new section 4.3a, the escalation of the landing weight from −30 to −45 and of the impact penalty from −3e-2 to −5e-2 with its threshold lowered from 900 to 800 newtons, the threshold change being the load bearing half because the impact term had gone inert rather than remaining strong and unheeded, its median touchdown now falling below its own threshold. It also declines, on measurement, a proposal to double the single support coefficient of `NoFlyWithGrace`, that branch being true on 99.8 percent of steps and therefore saturated in both arms of the comparison it would have to discriminate, and it names `feet_air_time_positive_biped` as the term that actually prices double support out, since it gates its whole reward on single stance and so charges 2.741 per second for the transfer interval. A methodological finding recorded there governs every future comparison, that the tracking error metrics do not converge downward while a curriculum is active, so runs must be compared at matched iteration counts with the curriculum values verified equal.

Revised a third time on 2026-08-05, run `2026-08-03_11-19-11` having completed at 29103 iterations and been re-evaluated, which the new section 4.2b reads against its predecessors at matched budget for the first time. Phase 1b holds and holds more comfortably, the touchdown speed having fallen further to 0.89 metres per second, but Phase 2 must be recorded as FAILED, its double support fraction having decayed from 11.62 percent at eleven thousand iterations to 7.86 against a gate of 12, so the provisional pass of section 4.2a is withdrawn. The pass establishes in two independent halves that the set point clearance term is an attractor and not merely a defective maximiser, its training income rising 36 percent over exactly the interval in which every measure of swing shape deteriorated, while its value reconstructed from kinematics is a third lower on the better profile than on the two worse ones, so the term pays more for the plateau and the policy climbs toward it whenever budget remains. That is the argument for Phase 3 stated in the currency of what a policy actually does.

Two matters recorded there require action before the next run. The working tree does NOT implement Phase 3, `foot_clearance_reward_v3` having no callers while the active term is `foot_clearance_reward_v2` at weight 20.0, which is the pre Phase 1b value, and the landing weight of −45 and the 800 newton impact threshold are live beside it, which is the one pairing section 4.3a expressly forbids. And the completed run's play dump is contaminated by exactly those three tree weights, detected by dividing a logged reward by the value reconstructed from kinematics and recovering an implied weight of 19.98 against a trained 12.0, so every reward figure in the section was rescaled before use and the same check must precede any future reading. The impact threshold floor also moves from 800 to 850 newtons, the steady stance force distribution having risen with the straight legged posture, and the tracking caution of section 4.2a is reversed, this run having improved its planar error against a tightening curriculum where its predecessor had not.

### GAIT_STATISTICS_PLAN.md

The specification of a pipeline that converts the analysis the gait work stream has performed by hand into an instrument it performs on load. Its motivating observation is that every pass since the twentieth has computed its figures from the numpy dump directly, which is what produced the durable results of the stream, and that each pass re-derived its instruments in a throwaway script none of which survives, so that a figure quoted in one pass cannot be reproduced in another and a comparison across runs is a manual assembly rather than a table.

The document is in three parts. It surveys the literature on the measurement of a gait rather than on its specification, drawing principally on clinical gait analysis and the humanoid balance line, and adds fourteen entries to `../context/literature.md` covering the stability margins, the symmetry and smoothness indices, the variability coefficients and the angular momentum regulation result. It then corroborates against the record every statistic the request that motivated it named, finding six of the seven families already computed somewhere in the stream and the seventh, the error between the expected and the measured base position, never computed because it is not computable from a dump carrying no orientation, and it enumerates twenty three further statistics the record computed which the request did not name. And it specifies the implementation in five ordered changes, a reader that reconstructs an experiment from the `params/env.yaml` the run itself dumped, an extension of the dump carrying the orientation, the actuator limits, the episode boundaries, the reward weights and the gait clock, a robot agnostic numpy only module implementing fifteen families of statistic, the wiring that writes a third dump beside the two the play already produces, and a dashboard window presenting the result as a table indexed by quantity and by statistic with one column per experiment.

The revision of 2026-08-05 carries eight changes taken from review and they alter the shape of the deliverable rather than its scope. The statistics module is `stats.py` rather than `statistics.py`, since the latter shadows the standard library module `rsl_rl` imports. The dump's leading singleton axis is abolished at its source rather than compensated for in each consumer, which deletes the unwrapping half of `get_env_data` in all four dashboards and is paired with a load time normalisation that keeps every historical dump readable. The contact force channel is moved onto the maximum over the sensor's force history, in the manner of the impact reward, rather than a second channel being added beside it, so every force figure the record carries becomes a lower bound against a fresh run. The metadata key tuple acquires a single home in `stats.py`. The `SOLE_OFFSETS` repair is withdrawn and deferred to the TRON1 correction pass, recorded as a defect deliberately left standing. And the parameter reader is new, its warrant being the discovery that `params/env.yaml` parses whole without importing Isaac Lab and carries the weight, the parameters and the import path of every reward term in the order `rewards.npy` records them, which makes a stored run replayable under the configuration it was trained against rather than the one the working tree holds.

The revision of 2026-08-06 carries six further changes from a second review and their common effect is to narrow the pipeline to one dump format and one computation site. The dumps of the record are to be regenerated, so every provision for reading the legacy shape is withdrawn, the `normalise_legacy_dump` helper and the dashboards' load time normalisation together. The statistics are computed only by `play.py`, so `stats.py` has one importer and the dashboards read the record set the play wrote rather than recomputing it. `rewards.npy` becomes a mandatory member of a dump, written unconditionally and read unconditionally, its absence raising. And the replay is configured from the run's own parameters on every invocation rather than under a flag, which is a deliberate departure from rule 4 of `../CLAUDE.md` taken under review direction and argued in the plan's section 4.4.

Two of its sections stand somewhat apart from the implementation and are the reason to read it even where the pipeline is not the subject. Section 5 gives, for every family, what the statistic means, what the record has taken it to mean, what it should be taken to mean, and the value a well behaved biped should exhibit, each grounded in the pass that established it. Section 6 reduces the whole of the stream's method to guidelines and then to a sixteen point checklist, covering what must be established before a statistic is computed, what makes two runs comparable, why the behaviour and the budget must be read together, the six known exploit signatures, and the five properties any change must preserve.

The plan was executed in full on 2026-08-06 and the outcome is recorded in section 11 of `../context/gait_metrics.md`. ### GAIT_STRATEGY.md

The first strategy document of the SD_BRS1 gait work stream, diagnosing a gait in which the robot tracked its heading while walking in a deep crouch with the torso pitched forward and the feet swept along the ground rather than lifted. Its diagnosis is the origin of several facts the later documents assume, that the foot clearance reward measured a point which stands above the ground when the foot is flat and therefore paid for the shuffle, that the same imported foot radius constant infected a second term, that the sparse air time reward acted as a negative barrier punishing every exploratory short step, that nothing in the reward set opposed leaning, that the all zero default pose rested at the straight leg singularity, that the proximal actuators were drastically underdamped, and that nothing in the stack expressed left right symmetry. It then sets out a phased strategy, a plant and pose phase carrying no reward changes, a reward and termination repair phase, a cadence phase closing the parked foot exploit, a symmetry phase, and an escalation phase.

Phases 0, 1, and 1b are implemented, and the symmetry phase was delegated to `SYMMETRY_PLAN.md` and implemented there. The escalation phase was never reached, because the work stream had by then moved on to the naturalness defects that `NATURAL_GAIT_PLAN.md` treats. Read this document for the diagnosis, which remains the clearest statement of why the SD_BRS1 reward set was originally miscalibrated, and read the later documents for the present state.

### CMAES_DESIGN_GENERATOR.md

The theoretical foundation and implementation plan for replacing the placeholder random design generator with an optimiser that learns from the per individual fitness signal. It surveys genetic algorithms in design optimisation, develops evolution strategies through to covariance matrix adaptation with the mathematics given in full, documents the interface of the pycma library with sample usage, dissects the existing random generator, and then specifies the new generator class and its integration. It is the longest treatment of the outer loop mathematics in the workspace and the correct reference for why covariance matrix adaptation was chosen over the alternatives.

The plan is implemented, the generator classes exist and drive every co-optimisation run. Two qualifications apply. It also contains one erratum, it states that the library applies a penalty based boundary handler, whereas `../context/cmaes.md` section 4 establishes by direct reading that the default is a saturating transform, a difference that matters because the two handlers bias the search differently near a bound.

### HIMLOCO_INTEGRATION_PLAN.md

The compatibility analysis and integration plan for the hybrid internal model implementation, written to reconcile the interface its runner expects with the interface that Isaac Lab and the vendored reinforcement learning library provide. It enumerates the members the runner accesses which the standard wrapper does not supply, the signature mismatches in the step return and the observation format, and the defects it found in the vendored implementation, and it then proposes an inheritance based bridge in three steps together with the configuration requirements.

This document was formerly named `TODO_himloco.md`, renamed for the same reason as the co-optimisation plan.

### LIPM_REWARD.md

A survey and implementation plan for a stability reward guided by the linear inverted pendulum model, motivated by the observation that the co-optimisation training signal exhibits a variance that never contracts across tens of thousands of iterations even after the design search has converged. It surveys the means by which model based information reduces sample complexity, develops the pendulum model on flat ground and then its inclined surface and rough terrain variants, treats the mathematics of sample complexity and the mechanisms for injecting a model into learning, and then specifies the reward. Its most useful structural observation is that the single design dependent parameter of the reward, the pendulum height, is an affine function of the very link scales the outer loop optimises, so the reward is design aware at negligible cost. It also records a correction to the source paper, whose stated reward equation omits the negative exponent that its own codomain requires, so an implementation must negate.

No part of this plan is implemented. No reward term of this kind exists in the reward module or in any environment configuration. The implementation seams the plan identifies were verified in the live code when it was written, and are recorded in the LIPM section of `../context/knowledge_base.md`, so the plan remains executable. The plan itself notes the limit of its ambition, that the reward cannot repair the curriculum non-stationarity and fitness attribution defects of the outer loop, which are the province of `COPT_INVESTIGATION_PLAN.md`.

## Conventions

A plan names its target as a file path and a symbol, and quotes surrounding code to locate an edit. Those quotations were taken from the sources as they stood when the plan was written and the line numbers have since drifted, so an edit should be located by symbol name and the quoted code treated as corroboration rather than as an address.

Cross references within this directory use the bare filename. References to the context documents take the form `../context/NAME.md`, and references to the two architecture documents at the workspace root take the form `../NAME.md`.

Where a plan has been implemented, the fact of implementation and any divergence from the plan is recorded in the corresponding context document rather than in the plan, so that the plan remains a statement of intent and the context record remains the statement of fact.

### QUADRUPED_INTEGRATION_PLAN.md

The plan for the project's first non bipedal robot, and for the rename that its arrival makes necessary. It divides into two halves. The first completes the move of the extension package from `exts/bipedal_locomotion/bipedal_locomotion/` to `environments/environments/`, a move already staged in the index at the level of the directories and of `setup.py` but not at the level of the forty one import statements, the extension manifest, the isort configuration and the three `djinn` installation lines that still address the old package name, and it enumerates every one of those sites. The second adds the quadruped described by `my_design.xml`, a machine of twelve actuated joints on 0.213 metre segments standing at a corrected 0.292 metres and massing a corrected 15.837 kilogrammes.

Its factual contributions are four. It audits every inertial entry of the supplied MuJoCo model against its declared primitive, establishes from the thigh that the leg segments were dimensioned as cylinders at a uniform density of exactly 700 kilogrammes per cubic metre, and finds four departures from that convention which the plan corrects in the MuJoCo file and in the URDF together rather than recording, namely a calf carrying the thigh's mass and inertia, a geometry type declaring capsules where the inertias are cylindrical, an abduction axis unmirrored between left and right where TRON1 mirrors it, and a standing keyframe 0.022 metres too low because the foot sphere's frame lies at its centre rather than at its sole. It computes the effective inertia at each joint through the parallel axis theorem at both the nominal crouch and full extension, finding the abduction joint the heaviest rather than the lightest as the biped intuition would suggest. It rejects the MuJoCo file's proportional gain of 100 as a modelling convenience with no hardware behind it, and derives both gains instead, the stiffness from the stance torque each joint must hold within half the action scale and the damping from a uniform damping ratio of 0.6, arriving at 40, 40 and 50 newton metres per radian against 1.2, 1.1 and 0.9, which places the three natural frequencies at 6.46, 7.02 and 10.15 hertz and leaves the action scale of 0.25 clear of all nine of its bounds. And it contributes to the joint control literature of this project two treatments that document lacks, the derivation of a proportional gain from a stance load and the derivation of a velocity range from the motor's torque speed characteristic, the slew rate the action scale demands, the transient peak of the second order response and the kinematics of swing at the target gait frequency, correcting in passing the peak velocity bound of `../tron1-rl-isaaclab-cozum/context/joint_control_analysis.md`, which overestimates by a factor near two and a half.

Its literature survey governs the reward configuration alone, on the ground that every other part of the biped configuration is robot agnostic in its construction. It establishes the canonical weight of each retained term against the ANYmal, Go2, A1 and Go1 configurations, identifies where the biped's tuned budget has drifted from that consensus by up to two orders of magnitude, and argues that the drift be closed only in part, because every reference carrying the canonical penalties also clips the summed return at zero and this framework does not. It rejects the specification's proposed air time threshold of zero, that value removing the branch of the term that penalises a hanging foot, and retunes the threshold to the swing duration of a two hertz trot. The result is seventeen terms, the sixteen the specification enumerates plus an abduction deviation penalty.

Three bodies of work are deliberately excluded and are to be planned independently, each recorded as a defect with its full scope so that the later plan need not rediscover it. The co-optimisation design generator is keyed to the biped's link and joint names at six places and needs a robot specification argument, the environment side being complete and verified. The evaluation script carries SD_BRS1's foot names as module level constants and assumes exactly two feet, and is non functional for TRON1 today, so it is left untouched beyond the rename. And the swing clearance reward the survey recommends measures a world frame height and is therefore correct on flat ground alone, so the term is omitted entirely rather than enabled on one terrain family and zeroed on the other, pending a variant that measures against the height scanner. The naming hazard the first draft recorded is closed rather than deferred, every quadruped class taking a `Quadruped` prefix so that no name collides with the LimX pointfoot biped's, and the new configuration module joining `cfg/__init__.py` ahead of the existing three star imports so that no present name resolution changes.

### QUADRUPED_DEGENERATE_GAIT.md

The first investigation of the quadruped that `QUADRUPED_INTEGRATION_PLAN.md` delivered, opened when the run at `IsaacLab/logs/rsl_rl/quadruped_flat/2026-08-25_10-15-10` produced a policy that supports itself on a reduced set of limbs while continuing to track the commanded velocity. Unlike the plans above it, this document is diagnostic before it is prescriptive, its section 6 deliberately left as a placeholder, because the purpose of its section 5 is to collapse a ranked list of nine candidate causes to one and a remedy written before that collapse would be a remedy for a fault that had not been located.

Three corrections to the reading of the run are established in its section 1 and matter to anyone consulting the log tree. The run trained on the rough terrain generator despite being filed under `quadruped_flat`, the directory name being a fixed experiment string rather than a record of the task launched. The video the observation rests upon, `rl-video-step-410000.mp4`, is iteration 16400 rather than a converged policy, one unit of the TensorBoard step axis being one optimisation iteration and the video counter advancing by twenty five per iteration. And the run had been declining for twelve thousand iterations by that point, having peaked at a mean return of 646 near iteration 5275 before stopping at 17443 of a configured thirty thousand.

The measurement that most sharpens the diagnosis contradicts the natural reading of the video. Converting each logged reward series back to the physical quantity its term integrates, by the factor of `episode_length_s` over the product of weight and control period, gives a mean trunk tilt between 5.82 and 10.23 degrees across the whole run, which excludes a sustained rear onto the hind pair, since a thirty degree rear would register a projected gravity magnitude of 0.5 against the 0.178 measured. What the same conversion does show is root mean square vertical velocity rising from 0.313 to 1.290 metres per second and roll and pitch rate from 1.18 to 2.15 radians per second. The failure is a collapse of the support pattern under a level trunk with violent vertical motion, which places it in the hopping family rather than the rearing family and changes which remedies of the literature apply.

Two findings survive whatever the experiments return. The `feet_air_time` term has been negative at every one of the 17267 logged iterations and has never once entered the region it was written to reward, because its floor of 0.25 seconds is above the air time the machine's cadence attains, and because a foot that never lands triggers no touchdown and is charged exactly zero, so the term's three outcomes are ordered with a retracted limb strictly better than a limb cycling at the achieved cadence. And the prior belief that framed the investigation, that actuator parameterisation is not the cause, is upheld by measurement rather than assumed, every actuator quantity computed coming out close between this machine and the Go1 or favouring the Go1, and the heavy leg fraction that looked most likely to matter conferring less centre of mass authority than the Go1's lighter and more distally distributed legs.

Its literature grounding is cluster 17 of `../context/literature.md` for the reward budget and the new cluster 19 for the degenerate gait material, and its evidence is preserved at `../context/artefacts/quadruped_flat-2026-08-25_10-15-10/` because the log tree that produced it is transient. Phase 1 of its section 5 costs no training at all, closing part of defect 7 of `QUADRUPED_INTEGRATION_PLAN.md` by registering a quadruped foot profile in the evaluation script and then dumping five existing checkpoints through the gait statistics pipeline, whose per foot stance duty is already generic over the number of feet.

The second round of 2026-09-02 replaced that section 5 entirely, because the measurement it existed to obtain has since been taken. Four surfaces were read for each of two later runs, the numpy evaluation dumps, the complete TensorBoard scalar records, the training and play videos, and the dumped configurations, and the evidence is preserved at `../context/artefacts/quadruped_flat-2026-09-01_07-22-37/` and `../context/artefacts/quadruped_flat-2026-09-02_08-53-04/`. The new section 5 is a five phase remediation programme rather than a diagnostic one, its phases 1 and 2 run as two arms of one experiment because neither is sufficient alone, phase 1 arresting the optimisation collapse and restoring the reward budget from published figures and phase 2 adding the only construction surveyed that forbids a retracted limb by arithmetic rather than by a threshold a large enough excess can evade.
